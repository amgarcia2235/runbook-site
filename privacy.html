Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

Short version: Daily Runbook does not collect any data. There is no account, no server, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. What the app records about your day stays on your iPhone.

What the app records, and where it stays

When you turn these features on, the app records on your device only:

  • When you leave and arrive at the home and work places you set (location, using iOS region monitoring).
  • Places you stop and the name of the nearest business from Apple Maps (location, using iOS visit monitoring).
  • When you were driving (Motion and Fitness, using the phone's motion sensors).
  • Your answers in the app, such as what a stop was for, and your checklist.

This log is stored in the app's own storage on your iPhone. It is not uploaded, synced to iCloud or shared with anyone. Deleting the app deletes it.

Apple Health

With your permission, the app reads sleep, steps and environmental noise readings from Apple Health to show them next to your day. Health data is only read; it is never written to disk by the app, never stored in iCloud, never sent anywhere and never used for advertising or data mining.

Calendar and Reminders

With your permission, the app shows today's calendar events and your open reminders, and can add or complete a reminder you choose. This stays within Apple's Calendar and Reminders on your device and your own iCloud account.

Lookups sent to Apple

To show the weather, the app sends the location of the home or work place you set to Apple's WeatherKit service. To show drive time and place names, it sends locations to Apple Maps. These requests go to Apple, are governed by Apple's privacy policy, and are not linked to you by Daily Runbook.

Notifications

Leave-by and walk reminders are local notifications created on your device. No push service is used.

Children

Daily Runbook is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
