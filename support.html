Help for Daily Runbook on iPhone and iPad.

Get in touch: open an issue at github.com/amgarcia2235/runbook-site/issues. Please do not include health details or addresses.

Getting started

  1. Open Settings in the app and set your work hours.
  2. Tap Set to here for Home while you are home, and for Work while you are at work.
  3. Turn on Trip logging and choose Always Allow for location, so times log with the app closed.
  4. Turn on Drive detection and allow Motion and Fitness.
  5. Tap Connect Apple Health, and connect Calendar and Reminders if you want them.

Want to look around first? Settings > Show a sample day fills the app with made-up data.

Common questions

A time did not log. iOS decides when location events are delivered. Check that location access is set to Always for Daily Runbook and that Background App Refresh is on. You can always tap a row in Day plan to mark it done.

A break or meal is wrong. Tap Not a break on the Work tab, or pick what a stop was on Today.

No sleep or steps. Check Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > Daily Runbook and turn on the categories.

Weather. Weather data is provided by Apple Weather.
